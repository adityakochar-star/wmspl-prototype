What is a hybrid operating theatre?

A hybrid operating theatre is a surgical room that combines a fully equipped OT with fixed advanced imaging, such as an angiography system or CT, so open surgery and image-guided minimally invasive procedures can happen in the same room. It needs imaging integration, radiation protection, airflow control and reliable power support, plus space for the imaging equipment and team. WMSPL's hybrid OT projects include University Hospital Ostrava, with integrated steel furniture and modular infrastructure, and one hybrid OT with complete steel furnishing at Hospital Sosna in Třinec, both in the Czech Republic.

Does WMSPL build modular cath labs?

Yes. WMSPL builds modular cath labs and imaging suites with cleanroom-grade systems, radiation protection, airflow control and power support. Its cath lab-linked project at Nanavati Max Super Speciality Hospital included modular stainless-steel systems, medical flooring, doors, scrub stations, lighting and medical bed-head units. Imaging equipment is supplied by the imaging manufacturer; the room is planned around that equipment's site requirements.
