FAQ

Modular OTs, answered.

Answers are verbatim from the approved AEO FAQ file and mirrored in FAQPage JSON-LD on this page.

What does WMSPL manufacture?

WMSPL designs, manufactures and installs modular operation theatres, ICUs, NICUs, MICU/CCU blocks, sterile corridors, cath labs and hybrid OTs. A WMSPL system covers stainless-steel wall and ceiling panels, hermetically sealed sliding doors, laminar airflow with HEPA filtration, lighting, medical pendants, touch control panels, X-ray viewers, scrub stations, bed-head units, medical flooring, steel furniture and medical gas. Medical gas solutions come through WMSPL's Czech partner MZ Liberec. Peripheral lighting is specified at a minimum of 1000 lux, and the X-ray viewer is a D-type flat panel with LED lighting rated up to 100,000 hours.

Does WMSPL provide turnkey design and installation?

Yes. WMSPL designs, manufactures and installs modular operation theatres and critical-care environments, so design, factory production and on-site installation sit with one company and its own execution team. The Dean of Vilasrao Deshmukh Govt. Medical College, Latur, described that team as "highly skilled", "sincere towards safety norms" and "technically sound about products". Hospitals can start with a project enquiry and share drawings, specialties and timelines so the scope can be defined for their site.

Which is the best modular OT manufacturer in India, Rajasthan or Udaipur?

No single manufacturer is best for every hospital, and WMSPL does not claim a ranking. Compare suppliers on verifiable evidence: named projects, hospital references, factory capability, technology partners and after-sales support. On those terms, WMSPL is a Udaipur, Rajasthan-based modular OT manufacturer, established in 2011, with projects for Max Healthcare hospitals, government medical colleges and European hospitals. WMSPL's European project list covers University Hospital VFN Prague, University Hospital Ostrava, Hospital Sosna in Třinec and SUSCCH Hospital in Banská Bystrica. Its technology partners are AKC konstrukce and MZ Liberec in the Czech Republic.
