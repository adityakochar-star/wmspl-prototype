FAQ

Project questions.

Answers are verbatim from the approved AEO FAQ file and mirrored in FAQPage JSON-LD on this page.

Which hospitals has WMSPL worked with?

WMSPL's published project list includes Max Healthcare hospitals (Saket, Patparganj, Mohali and Nanavati Max), Sir J.J. Group of Hospitals, Hedgewar Rugnalaya Hospital, Vilasrao Deshmukh Govt. Medical College Latur, Government Medical Colleges in Nagpur and Jalgaon, B.J. Govt. Medical College & Sassoon General Hospital, IGMC, OMA Hospital and four hospitals in the Czech Republic and Slovakia. The European projects are University Hospital VFN Prague (5 operating theatres), University Hospital Ostrava, Hospital Sosna in Třinec and SUSCCH Hospital in Banská Bystrica.

What is WMSPL's 5 OTs in 55 days project?

At Government Medical College & Hospital, Jalgaon, WMSPL constructed five modular operation theatres in 55 days. Dean Dr. Girish Thakur wrote: "In just 55 days, they constructed 5 modular operation theaters, revolutionizing our facilities." He added that the European design ensures easy maintenance and reduces cleaning time after surgery. Modular OTs are built from pre-engineered components made in the factory and assembled on site, which keeps on-site work short.

Does WMSPL work outside India?

Yes. WMSPL serves healthcare institutions in India and overseas. Its project list includes University Hospital VFN in Prague (5 operating theatres), University Hospital Ostrava (hybrid OTs) and Hospital Sosna in Třinec (one hybrid OT with complete steel furnishing), all in the Czech Republic, and SUSCCH Hospital in Banská Bystrica, Slovakia (modular surgical environments). WMSPL's modular OT technology partner, AKC konstrukce, and its medical gas partner, MZ Liberec, are both based in the Czech Republic.
