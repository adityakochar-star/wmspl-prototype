Where is WMSPL based?

WMSPL is headquartered in Udaipur, Rajasthan, India. Its address is Plot No. B-437, Bhamashah Industrial Area, Road No. 18A, Kaladwas, Udaipur, Rajasthan. The company serves hospitals and medical colleges across India and overseas, and can be reached on +91 92518 59361 or +91 91191 19478, or by email at marketing@wmspl.co.in. The Udaipur address is where WMSPL's own manufacturing is based.

How do I get a quote for a modular OT?

Use the 'Plan your Modular OT' enquiry form on wmspl.co.in, call +91 92518 59361 or +91 91191 19478, WhatsApp +91 92518 59361, or email marketing@wmspl.co.in. Share your hospital name, city, project type, number of OTs or beds, timeline and any drawings, so the WMSPL team can scope a proposal. Cost depends on the number of rooms, the specialty and the systems specified, so proposals are prepared per project.

What after-sales support and maintenance does WMSPL provide?

WMSPL provides a 5-year warranty for its modular OTs. There is no AMC charge in years 1 and 2. For years 3 to 5, AMC is 2% of the order value per year. From year 6 to year 15, an optional extension costs 4% of the order value per year. Ask for the warranty and maintenance terms in writing as part of the proposal.
