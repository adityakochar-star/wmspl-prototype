What is a modular ICU?

A modular ICU is an intensive care unit built from factory-made, pre-engineered components assembled on site: stainless-steel wall systems, medical flooring, door systems, scrub stations and bed-head units that bring medical gas and power to every bed. Like a modular OT, it needs less on-site civil work and is designed for easy cleaning and infection control. The same approach is used for NICUs, MICU/CCU blocks, recovery areas and sterile corridors.

Does WMSPL build ICUs, NICUs and MICU/CCU blocks?

Yes. Alongside operation theatres, WMSPL builds critical-care environments. For Sir J.J. Group of Hospitals it developed a 77-bed MICU & CCU with modular stainless-steel systems, medical flooring, door systems, scrub stations and bed-head units, and implemented surgery, ICU, NICU and recovery units over multiple phases. Hedgewar Rugnalaya Hospital's project included an ICU and a sterile corridor. Hospitals planning a critical-care block can specify the number of beds in WMSPL's enquiry form.
