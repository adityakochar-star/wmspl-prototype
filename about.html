Who is WMSPL?

Workspace Metal Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (WMSPL) is a Udaipur-based company, established in 2011, that designs, manufactures and installs modular operation theatres and critical-care environments in India and overseas, with modular OT technology from Czech partner AKC konstrukce and medical gas solutions from MZ Liberec. WMSPL serves multi-specialty hospitals, medical colleges, diagnostic centres, ICUs, NICUs and specialised surgical departments. Its vision is to be "Truly Inventive".

Who are WMSPL's technology partners?

WMSPL has two technology partners in the Czech Republic: AKC konstrukce s.r.o., based in Rožnov pod Radhoštěm, for modular operation theatre technology, and MZ Liberec, based in Rudník, for medical gas solutions. They are partners, not owners: WMSPL manufactures its systems in Udaipur, India, and installs projects in India and overseas. More about each partner is available at akcmed.cz and mzliberec.cz.
